About Us

Your Journey to Becoming a Professional Barista Starts Here

One O One Coffee & Barista School is a premier coffee training institution located in Bhaktapur, Nepal. Founded in 2019, we have been committed to transforming coffee enthusiasts into skilled professionals through comprehensive barista training programs.

With over 7,000+ graduated students, we take pride in our track record of producing industry-ready baristas. Our three convenient locations at Suryabinayak, Gatthaghar, and Baneshwor make quality coffee education accessible to everyone.

We offer both indoor and outdoor training sessions, providing hands-on experience with state-of-the-art equipment in real-world settings. Our experienced instructors ensure you receive the best training in the art and science of coffee.
